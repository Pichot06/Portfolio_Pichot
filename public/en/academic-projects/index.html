Plant Manager

Context As part of our Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing degree at IUT Nice, Thomas Gomes and I developed an automatic plant-watering system. The smart plant measures soil moisture, ambient temperature and CO₂ levels, then automatically starts watering according to the collected data. I was responsible for the electronic architecture, sensor testing, PCB design with DesignSpark, and 3D modelling and printing. Thomas developed the web supervision interface. Drone trajectory planning

Introduction For this project, we developed an algorithm allowing a drone to follow a predefined trajectory smoothly and accurately. The goal was to generate waypoints while respecting acceleration, maximum speed and motion-smoothing constraints. Trajectories We studied fifth-degree polynomial trajectories and LSPB trajectories (Linear Segment with Parabolic Blends). LSPB trajectories combine linear segments with parabolic transitions. They are predictable, provide smooth speed changes and adapt well to robotics constraints.
