## Context

As part of our Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing studies at the University of Nice, Thomas Gomes and I developed an automatic plant-watering system.

The system uses a Raspberry Pi Zero 2W, analog and digital sensors, and several actuators. It measures soil moisture, ambient temperature and carbon dioxide levels, then automatically triggers watering according to the collected data. The whole system is monitored through a web interface.

I was responsible for the electronic architecture, sensor unit testing, PCB design with DesignSpark, and the 3D modelling and printing of the enclosures. Thomas developed the complete web supervision interface.
