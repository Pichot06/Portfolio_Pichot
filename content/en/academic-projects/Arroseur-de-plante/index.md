## Context

As part of our Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing degree at IUT Nice, Thomas Gomes and I developed an automatic plant-watering system.

The smart plant measures soil moisture, ambient temperature and CO₂ levels, then automatically starts watering according to the collected data. I was responsible for the electronic architecture, sensor testing, PCB design with DesignSpark, and 3D modelling and printing. Thomas developed the web supervision interface.
