As part of my second year of the BUT GEII degree at IUT Nice, I designed a standalone Class-D audio amplifier. The project combines Bluetooth signal processing, analog conditioning with operational amplifiers, PWM generation in VHDL on a DE10-Lite board, and amplification through an H-bridge.

I designed two electronic boards, modelled the system in 3D, and built a wooden speaker inspired by Marshall's design. The project also includes LC filtering, dynamic LED lighting, LTspice and MATLAB simulations, and real-world tests.
