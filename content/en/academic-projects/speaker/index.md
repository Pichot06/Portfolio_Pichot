As part of my second year of Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing studies, I designed a fully autonomous Class D audio amplifier.

The project combines Bluetooth signal processing, analog conditioning with operational amplifiers, PWM signal generation in VHDL on a DE10-Lite board, and H-bridge amplification. I designed two electronic boards, modelled the system in 3D, and built a wooden speaker inspired by Marshall designs. The project also includes LC filtering, dynamic LED lighting, LTSpice and MATLAB simulations, and real-world validation.
