### Hello, I am Alexandre

I am an engineering student at INSA Hauts-de-France, with a strong interest in home automation, robotics and new technologies. My goal is to design intelligent solutions that make everyday life safer, more comfortable and more sustainable.

### My background

My studies in Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing gave me solid foundations in:

- Electronics
- Automation
- Industrial computing
- Electrical engineering

This demanding environment has allowed me to work on practical projects and develop a rigorous approach to teamwork.

### What drives me

I enjoy electronic prototyping, embedded programming and building systems around microcontrollers. Robotics particularly interests me because it combines mechanics, electronics and embedded intelligence.

I regularly work with:

- Arduino, ESP32, STM32 and Mbed microcontrollers
- Embedded programming
- Sensor integration
- Home automation systems with Home Assistant

I am currently developing personal projects that combine connected objects and autonomous robotics, with the aim of creating useful and modular solutions for the real world.
